Backcountry Books – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This policy explains how Backcountry Books ("the App"), operated by Brock Zollinger ("the Developer"), handles data accessed from QuickBooks Online.

1. What data the App accesses

With your authorization, the App reads accounting data from your QuickBooks Online company, such as company information, financial reports (for example, profit and loss), transactions, invoices, and bills.

2. How the data is used

Data is used only to produce financial reports, forecasts, and analysis for the business that authorized access. It is not used for advertising.

3. Sharing

The Developer does not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties, except where required by law.

4. Storage and security

Data is processed on the Developer's own computer. Access tokens and credentials are kept private and are never published or shared. Any data saved for analysis is stored locally and deleted when no longer needed.

5. Your choices

You can disconnect the App from your QuickBooks Online company at any time, which stops all further access. You may also ask the Developer to delete any of your data they hold.

6. Changes to this policy

The Developer may update this policy. The "last updated" date above will change when it does.

7. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to brockzollinger@gmail.com.
